FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about rentals, fittings, and more.

We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks before your event for individual fittings, and 6–8 weeks for group or wedding orders. This ensures enough time for alterations and any special requests.
Our Essential package starts at $129 and includes a suit or tuxedo, shirt, tie, and basic alterations. The Premium package ($199) adds a pocket square, shoe rental, and premium alterations. Group packages start at $159/person for 6+ members.
Yes! Free modifications and cancellations are available up to 7 days before your event. Changes within the 7-day window may incur a small fee. Contact us immediately if your plans change and we'll do our best to accommodate.
We do offer rush services depending on availability. Same-day rentals are possible for in-stock sizes with a rush fee. Please call us directly at (212) 555-7890 for the fastest assistance.
We schedule a pickup day 1–2 days before the event with a final try-on. If anything needs adjusting, our on-site tailors handle same-day alterations at no extra charge. Your satisfaction is our guarantee.
Yes, we ship nationwide! For out-of-state group orders, members can submit their measurements online. We ship the fitted ensemble to your address with a prepaid return label included. Standard shipping is 5–7 business days.
