Who We Are

A Legacy of Impeccable Style

Black Tie House was founded in 1987 on Madison Avenue with a simple belief: every man deserves to feel extraordinary on the days that matter most. What started as a small tuxedo shop has grown into New York's most trusted name in formalwear.

Our founder, Anthony Marchetti, a third-generation Italian tailor, brought old-world craftsmanship to the modern gentleman. Today, his legacy continues through a team of master tailors who share his passion for precision, quality fabrics, and the perfect fit.

From intimate weddings to grand galas, from proms to presidential inaugurations — we've had the privilege of dressing thousands of clients for their most memorable occasions.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Every stitch, every fitting, every interaction is guided by these principles.

Masterful Craftsmanship

Every garment is inspected, pressed, and adjusted by hand. We uphold the traditions of Italian tailoring passed down through generations.

Personal Service

From the first consultation to the final fitting, you work with a dedicated style advisor who understands your vision and occasion.

Premium Quality

We source the finest fabrics — Italian wool, Egyptian cotton, silk linings — from the same mills used by world-class fashion houses.

Group Excellence

Wedding parties, proms, corporate events — we've perfected the art of coordinating style for groups of any size, stress-free.

Sustainable Fashion

Renting formalwear is inherently sustainable. We extend each garment's life through expert care, reducing waste in the fashion industry.

Fit Guarantee

Not satisfied with the fit? Our tailors will re-adjust your garment on the spot — at no additional charge. Your confidence is our commitment.

Our Journey

Milestones That Define Us

1987

The Beginning

Anthony Marchetti opens the first Black Tie House on Madison Avenue with 50 hand-selected tuxedos and a dream of redefining formalwear in New York.

1995

Wedding Division Launch

We introduce our dedicated wedding coordination service, quickly becoming the go-to for New York's most prestigious venues and wedding planners.

2005

Designer Partnerships

We secure exclusive partnerships with top Italian and British designers, bringing runway-quality formalwear to our rental collection for the first time.

2015

Going Digital

Launch of our online platform with virtual consultations and nationwide shipping, making premium formalwear accessible beyond New York City.

Today

The Gold Standard

With 50,000+ clients served, a 4.9-star rating, and a flagship showroom on Madison Ave, Black Tie House continues to set the standard for specialty formalwear.
